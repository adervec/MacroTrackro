## The ingredient guide

The reference values in `src/data/kb.ts` are facts drawn from public sources: the US/Canada
Dietary Reference Intakes (National Academies) as published in the NIH Office of Dietary
Supplements fact sheets, FDA and Health Canada advisories, and International Society of Sports
Nutrition position stands. The wording is the maker's own. It is general information, not
medical advice.
